What is Insomnia?

Insomnia is a common sleep disorder that can make it hard to fall asleep, hard to stay asleep, or cause you to wake up too early and not be able to get back to sleep. It can drain your energy level and drastically affect your mood, health, and performance at work or school.

1in3

people in our survey group of 15 reported living with chronic insomnia.

Common Causes

Habitual causes of chronic insomnia include:

  • Stress: Concerns about work, school, health, or finances.
  • Poor Sleep Habits: Irregular schedules, screen usage right before bed.
  • Travel or Work Schedules: Disruptions to your circadian rhythm.
  • Substance Use: Caffeine, nicotine, or alcohol late in the day.

Frequent Symptoms

People suffering from insomnia may experience:

  • Difficulty falling asleep at night.
  • Waking up during the night or waking up too early.
  • Not feeling well-rested after a night's sleep.
  • Daytime tiredness, sleepiness, irritability, or anxiety.

Types of Insomnia

It is mainly categorized based on its duration:

  • Acute (Short-term): Lasts for days or weeks, often caused by stress or traumatic events.
  • Chronic: Occurs at least 3 nights a week for 3 months or longer without a direct apparent medical cause.

— Top Response #3 (Late-day stimulant intake)
