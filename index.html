Light in the Darkness

Insomnia can make the night feel endless, but remember: the longest shadows always precede the dawn. If you suffer in silence tonight, do not despair; your mind and body are searching for peace, so be patient with yourself. And if you sleep peacefully, cherish the rest and show empathy to those who struggle in the dark. No night lasts forever.
